# Brag Plan: FlowFoundry AI Solutions

## What is this app?
FlowFoundry is a Vijayawada-based engineering studio that builds AI agents, voice automation,
workflow automation, custom software and integrations — and its flagship product, LeadPulz,
is an AI voice agent that answers the phone, qualifies the lead, updates the CRM and books the meeting.

## The angle
**The pipeline runs itself.** The hero of the site already shows a five-step "Intelligent Workflow"
card: New Lead → AI Agent → Business Logic → CRM + Calendar → Action. That *is* the product — not
a diagram of it. The video plays that pipeline forward in real time, one row lighting up after
another, until a meeting is booked with nobody touching a keyboard. Then it cuts to the LeadPulz
dashboard where the numbers prove it happened 627 times.

Specific to this project: the site's exact pipeline labels, exact stat values from the real
dashboard screenshot, the navy/cyan→violet palette, and the closing question the site actually asks.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Navy screen. A single enquiry notification slides in from the top, like a phone alert:
**"New Lead · Website enquiry"**. Then the site's headline lands underneath in light Poppins:
*Build intelligent systems around the way your **business works.*** (gradient on the last two words).
The viewer immediately has a lead sitting there and a promise about what happens to it.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The Intelligent Workflow card assembles itself: 5 rows arrive one by one, the **AI Agent** row
  glowing blue→violet, the **Action** row glowing emerald. Each row carries the site's own meta
  copy ("Engage & understand", "Qualify & route", "Sync business data", "Book & follow up").
- Three outcome chips pop in beneath: **Lead qualified · CRM updated · Meeting booked** — the last
  one is the payoff, timed to a strong cue.
- Hard cut to a white product frame: the real LeadPulz dashboard screenshot, with four stat tiles
  counting up over it — **627** calls completed · **75.0%** connection rate · **28.1%** qualification
  rate · **11.8%** meetings booked. "LeadPulz AI" wordmark above.

## Outro / punchline
Back to navy. The site's closing question, verbatim:
*What would you automate if your team had **more time?***
Then the FlowFoundry logo + wordmark, `flowfoundryai.in`, and a chip row:
AI Agents · Automation · Custom Software · Integrations.

## User flow worth showing
Entry → key action → result:
1. **A lead comes in** (website enquiry notification).
2. **The AI agent handles it** — engages, qualifies, routes, syncs CRM + calendar (the pipeline rows).
3. **Result: meeting booked** (outcome chips) — and the dashboard shows it at scale (627 calls, 11.8% booked).

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet premium B2B product film — the pipeline runs itself
- Interpretation: 4 scenes, 4–6s each, light-weight display type with generous tracking, soft
  crossfades, restraint everywhere except the two payoffs (Meeting booked / stat count-up).
  No hype words; only copy the site already uses.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 33.2s (services scene + narration added on request; over the 25s law deliberately)

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background (dark): `#0A1330` (site html/body + hero); ambient blurred orbs blue-500/30, violet-500/25, cyan-500/10; faint 64px grid at ~4% white
- Background (light product frame): `#ffffff` with `#f5f6f8` cloud surface, `#e6e8ef` borders
- Accent: brand gradient `linear-gradient(90deg, #3ac9ff 0%, #6161ff 52%, #9450fd 100%)`; violet `#6161ff`; cyan `#3ac9ff`; success emerald `#34d399` (emerald-400)
- Text on dark: `#ffffff` headings, `rgba(255,255,255,.72)` body, `rgba(255,255,255,.55)` labels
- Text on light: ink `#252830`, slate `#535768`, muted `#666c79`, quiet `#8a8f9b`
- Display font: Poppins 300 (hero, tracking -0.04em), Poppins 600 for card titles
- Body font: Poppins 400
- Eyebrow style: 12px, 600, 0.15em tracking, uppercase, violet (on dark: on-dark-quiet)
- Radii: cards 24px, inner panels 18px, chips 12px, pills 9999px
- Strongest visual element: the dark glass "Intelligent Workflow" card (border white/10, bg white/3,
  backdrop blur, numbered 01–05 tiles, primary row blue-500/10 with gradient tile, success row emerald)
- Real assets to use: `public/logo.png` (1254px square), `public/images/dashboard.jpeg` (1348x602 LeadPulz dashboard)

## Audio direction
- Role: warm, steady bed under a restrained edit
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (steady and clean, 110 BPM)
- Music treatment: start at 0, volume ~0.32, quick 0.4s fade-in, fade out over the final 1.2s under the logo
- Music cue guidance: preset read from `<skill-dir>/assets/music/cues/…vol-12….music-cues.md`.
  Strong cues to target: **8.74s** (pipeline card fully assembled / "Meeting booked" chip), **10.93s**
  (cut to LeadPulz dashboard), **17.47s** (closing question lands). Beat grid for the 5 pipeline rows
  around 4.39 → 7.09s (every other beat: 4.91, 5.34/6.00, 6.56, 7.09, 7.64 — hold each ≥0.8s settled).
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; music RMS/bass may breathe the navy ambient orbs and the glow of
  the AI Agent row. No waveform / equalizer / particle visuals.
- SFX posture: sparse, polished, low-HF-risk files only (2–5 cues total)
- Audio-coupled moments: notification slide-in (soft drop), pipeline rows (soft drop on first and last,
  not every row), "Meeting booked" chip (one bell), stat count-up (no SFX — let the music carry), logo landing (one soft impact)
- Restraint rule: never an SFX on every row; no aggressive impacts; SFX ≤ 0.6 volume; music never above 0.35.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook: a lead arrives — 4.5s (0.0–4.5)
Navy `#0A1330` with the site's faint grid and two blurred orbs (blue top-right, violet bottom-left).
At ~0.3s a small glass notification pill slides down from the top: a "01" tile, **New Lead**, meta
"Website enquiry", and a green "Active" dot. At ~1.2s the hero headline rises in beneath it, Poppins
300, ~72px, white: "Build intelligent systems around the way your" + gradient "business works."
Eyebrow above it: "AI • AUTOMATION • SOFTWARE". Holds settled until ~4.1s (headline is 10 words → 3s hold).
Sequential/interaction: yes — notification first, then headline; nothing else.
Audio intent: quiet arrival; the bed fades in under the notification.
Audio-coupled idea: one soft drop when the notification lands.
Music: bed starts at 0, fades in over 0.4s.
Transition mood: soft → Scene 2 (crossfade; the notification reads as row 01 of the workflow card)

### Scene 2 — The pipeline runs itself — 6.0s (4.5–10.5)
The dark glass "Intelligent Workflow" card (header "INTELLIGENT WORKFLOW / FlowFoundry automation
architecture", green "Active" pill) sits center-right; a small caption sits on the
left: "Conversations → Business logic → Data → Actions" (the site's four layers). The five rows arrive
one by one from the right (site's own motion: x:20→0, fade), each ~0.55s apart on the beat grid:
01 New Lead / Website enquiry · 02 **AI Agent** / Engage & understand (blue/violet gradient tile, blue
tint) · 03 Business Logic / Qualify & route · 04 CRM + Calendar / Sync business data · 05 **Action** /
Book & follow up (emerald). A thin gradient connector line grows between rows. Rows 1–5 land by ~7.3s
and hold ≥0.8s. Then three outcome chips pop in beneath: "Lead qualified" (~8.2s), "CRM updated" (~8.5s),
**"Meeting booked"** locked to the 8.74s strong cue, with a brief emerald glow. Hold to 10.5s.
Sequential/interaction: yes — 5 rows one by one, then 3 chips one by one.
Audio intent: momentum building to a satisfying click.
Audio-coupled idea: soft drop on row 01 and row 05 only; one bell on "Meeting booked". Rows may
subtly breathe with bass (audio-reactive glow on the AI Agent row).
Music: bed steady.
Transition mood: clean (hard cut near the 10.93s beat, slight scale-up of the card before cut) → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Proof: the LeadPulz dashboard — 6.0s (10.5–16.5)
Cut to a white product frame: eyebrow "FLAGSHIP PRODUCT" (violet), "LeadPulz AI" title, and the real
`dashboard.jpeg` inside a browser-style card (three traffic-light dots, 24px radius, card shadow),
Ken-Burns pushing in ~4% over the scene. Over the screenshot's stat tiles, four clean overlay tiles
count up in sequence (numbers in Poppins 500, ~56px ink; labels muted): **627** Calls completed →
**75.0%** Connection rate → **28.1%** Qualification rate → **11.8%** Meetings booked. Count-ups each
~0.7s, starting at 11.6s and staggered ~0.6s (alternate beats), all settled by ~14.6s and held to 16.5s.
The site's two floating chips appear at the corners: "AI AGENT · Lead qualified" (bottom-left, green dot)
and "ACTION · Meeting booked" (top-right).
Sequential/interaction: yes — 4 stat tiles count up one after another; two floating chips drift ±8px.
Audio intent: confident, proof-of-work; the music carries this scene.
Audio-coupled idea: none (count-ups without ticks — polished restraint). Optional: the card's shadow
presence breathes subtly with RMS.
Music: bed steady; cut lands near the 10.93s beat.
Transition mood: soft crossfade (0.7s) → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Outro: the question, then the mark — 5.5s (16.5–22.0)
Back to navy with the grid and orbs. The closing question rises in, Poppins 300, ~64px, centered:
"What would you automate if your team had" + gradient "more time?" — landing on the 17.47s strong cue,
held ≥2.4s (8 words). At ~19.4s it eases up and shrinks slightly; the FlowFoundry logo (`logo.png`)
+ "FlowFoundry AI Solutions" wordmark fade in beneath, then "flowfoundryai.in" and a quiet chip row:
AI Agents · Automation · Custom Software · Integrations. Hold to 21.0s. Music fades over the last 1.2s.
Sequential/interaction: yes — question, then logo lockup, then chip row.
Audio intent: settle and sign off.
Audio-coupled idea: one soft impact when the logo lands (~19.66s beat); nothing else.
Music: bed fades out 19.8→21.0s.
Transition mood: none — end.

**Scene durations: 4.5 + 6.0 + 6.0 + 5.5 = 22.0s** (scene 4 got one extra second so the logo lockup holds ≥1.3s after the question lifts)

**Music mood for this video:** steady, clean, corporate-warm (vol-12)
**Audio summary:** a single warm bed that fades in under a lead notification, ticks along with the pipeline assembling, lands one bell on "Meeting booked", carries the dashboard proof on its own, and fades under the logo with one soft final impact.
